# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sutelio serves individuals and workspace teams who plan, coordinate, and complete projects and tasks across web and NativePHP Mobile. They work in multiple workspaces with owner, administrator, and member responsibilities and need a dependable interface for daily task focus, project oversight, collaboration, notifications, calendar work, settings, and local data operations.

## Product Purpose

Sutelio is a trustworthy local-first workspace command center. It lets users organize projects, tasks, people, schedules, files, activity, and preferences without losing workspace isolation, while keeping the SQLite application operable, portable, and useful in empty, normal, failure, and high-volume states. Success means users can understand what needs attention, act with confidence, recover from errors, and move between web and mobile without relearning the interface.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, precise, and purposeful. The product should feel calm enough for sustained work, warm enough to be approachable, and rigorous enough that users trust destructive, security, and data-safety workflows.

## Anti-references

- Tutorial CRUD, generic admin templates, or ornamental SaaS dashboards that put decoration ahead of the task.
- Inconsistent controls, page-specific visual inventions, excessive card nesting, gratuitous motion, or modal-first interaction design.
- AI-generated visual tropes such as gradient text, decorative glass, repeated eyebrow scaffolding, oversized rounding, and border-plus-wide-shadow treatment on every surface.
- Interfaces that rely on color alone, hide important actions behind hover, or optimize only for English desktop use.

## Design Principles

1. Make the current task and next safe action immediately legible.
2. Earn trust through consistent language, predictable controls, honest states, and reversible workflows.
3. Prefer one shared system correction over repeated page-specific patches.
4. Keep density purposeful: show the information needed for decisions without turning every group into a card.
5. Treat localization, accessibility, responsive behavior, and failure recovery as the normal interface, not follow-up polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across English, Lithuanian, and Russian. Preserve keyboard and touch operation, screen-reader semantics, visible focus, 44-pixel primary touch targets, 200% zoom/reflow, non-color status cues, reduced-motion parity, forced-colors usability, readable contrast, and mobile-first layouts without horizontal page overflow.
